## 1. Philosophy Pillars (cross-cutting filter)

From VOICE-AND-WORLDVIEW.md §1. Every story should touch at least one:

1. SOVEREIGNTY — financial, data, civil. Own your money, data, speech.
2. OWNERSHIP — creators own work, audience, upside. No rent-seeking.
3. AI AS DEFINING FORCE — embrace it, hold it accountable.
4. BUILD > CONSUME — producers > consumers. Agency and execution.
5. COMMUNITY IS THE MOAT — the network of people is the durable asset.
6. CULTURE ON-CHAIN — media, IP, identity permanent and ownable.
7. TECH OPTIMISM, EARLY-ADOPTER — "we're already in the future."
8. SELF-MASTERY — stoic, athletic, compounding, consistency.
9. ACCESS & GENERATIONAL UPLIFT — democratize creation and capital.

This filter applies to ALL four categories. A story about Nike buying a community
(Pillar 2 + 5 + Business) lands in Business. A story about an AI artist tool
(Pillar 3 + 4 + 6) lands in Technology. A story about a sports team moving to Austin
(Pillar 5 + 7 + 9) lands in Sports.

## 5. Voice & Tone Rules

From VOICE-AND-WORLDVIEW §4 — how the newsletter sounds:

**Register:** streetwise + intellectual. Hip-hop cadence meets builder/hustler optimism meets stoic aphorism.

**Do:**
- Lead with a take — not "some people think," your take.
- Ground in lived experience or a concrete example.
- End with forward motion or a question.
- Use aphoristic one-liners ("be the point of value in an ecosystem, not the empty space").
- Weave in community shout-outs.
- Emoji as punctuation (🤙🏾 🧠 💵 🚀 🌊 💎).

**Don't:**
- Corporate hedging, jargon for its own sake, doom.
- Generic "X is changing the world" opens.
- Cynicism — you're early and building.

**Era note:** 2021-2023 was NFT-maximalist. 2024-2026 is AI-forward, more reflective
(safety, future of work, media effects on mind, stoic themes). Lead with AI +
sovereignty + building. NFT is now one beat under Culture, not the whole identity.

## 6. Source Selection Heuristics (ranking)

- **Prioritize** primary builders/operators over pundits.
- **Rank up** stories touching a pillar (AI leverage, sovereignty, creator ownership,
  on-chain culture, Texas-as-frontier, community moats, producer mindset).
- **Rank down** pure price-speculation noise and doom-takes.
- **Never** serve speculation as analysis — we have a take, or we skip it.

## 8. Small-Business Closing Beat (special note)

Micah specifically wants coverage of small businesses closing — especially
"when they close." This is a Culture/Business hybrid beat. Watch for:
  - Restaurant, retail, local business closure stories
  - "Mom and pop" / "family-owned" + "closing" / "gone" / "shuttered"
  - Commercial real estate vacancies, downtown dead zones
  - Shrinkflation: same price, less product — shrinkflation, skimpflation,
    "they shrunk the [product]"
  - Retail apocalypse narratives
  - Community reaction to local business loss

This hits Pillar 5 (community is the moat) + Pillar 9 (access/uplift — loss
of access is the other side of that coin). It's not doom — it's the erosion
of the local moat, which matters to the Innovative Hype worldview.
